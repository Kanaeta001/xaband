## PRODUCT OVERVIEW

The X-A Band is a safety identification bracelet for individuals with autism and special needs. Each band features a unique QR code that links to a parent-controlled profile with communication tips, sensory needs, and emergency contact information.

## DESIGN ELEMENTS

### 1. BASE BAND COLOR
- **Color:** Gold / Amber
- **Pantone:** 130 C (or closest match)
- **Hex:** #D97706
- **Meaning:** "Au" = chemical symbol for gold = first letters of "Autism"

### 2. EDGE ACCENT STRIPES (Top & Bottom)
- **Color:** Black
- **Pantone:** Black C
- **Hex:** #1A1A1A
- **Width:** 3-4mm on each edge
- **Method:** Debossed + filled OR co-molded silicone

### 3. HOLOGRAPHIC STRIP (Center)
- **Position:** Running horizontally through center of band
- **Width:** 6-8mm
- **Type:** Rainbow iridescent / holographic film
- **Method:** Heat transfer, embedded film, or holographic foil
- **Purpose:** Eye-catching, makes the band noticeable and recognizable

### 4. THE EMBRACE SYMBOL (Left side of QR)
- **Design:** Two curved lines embracing a center circle
- **Dimensions:** Approximately 15mm wide × 12mm tall
- **Colors:** 
  - Arms: Black (#1A1A1A) OR holographic
  - Center circle: Indigo (#4F46E5) with white inner circle
- **Method:** Debossed + filled, printed, or molded relief

### 5. QR CODE PLATE (Center)
- **Dimensions:** 25mm × 20mm (visible QR area)
- **Plate:** Black background (#1A1A1A)
- **QR Code:** White on black, high contrast
- **Method:** Laser-etched metal insert OR high-resolution print under resin
- **Note:** Each band has a UNIQUE QR code linking to individual profile

### 6. X-A BRANDING (Right side of QR)
- **Text:** "X-A"
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif (Inter Black or similar)
- **Color:** Black (#1A1A1A)
- **Size:** Approximately 10mm height
- **Method:** Debossed + filled

## QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

- **Waterproof:** IP67 rated (submersible)
- **Durability:** Must withstand daily wear, washing, swimming
- **Comfort:** Smooth edges, no sharp points, flexible
- **QR Scanning:** Must scan reliably from 10cm distance with standard smartphone
- **Skin-safe:** Hypoallergenic, non-toxic, no latex
- **Color fastness:** Colors must not fade with UV exposure or washing
