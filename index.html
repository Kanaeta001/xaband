Why We Built This

I've watched my autistic son struggle to order food while a cashier grew visibly impatient. I've seen the confusion on his face when strangers didn't understand why he needed more time. I've stepped in more times than I can count to explain, "He just needs a moment."

But I can't always be there. No parent can.

That's why X-A Band exists — a simple way to tell the world who he is and what he needs, even when I'm not beside him. Not a medical tag that labels him. A band that protects him with dignity.

This isn't a product. It's a mission. And it started with watching my son — and refusing to accept that he has to navigate this world without backup.

— Kanaeta, Founder of X-A Corp
The Solution

One Band. Instant Understanding.

X-A Band is a comfortable, waterproof silicone bracelet with a unique QR code. When scanned, it shows a parent-controlled profile with everything someone needs to know to help — no app required.

  • ✓ Waterproof silicone — survives showers, pools, everyday life
  • ✓ Unique QR code — links to a personal profile
  • ✓ Parent-controlled info — you decide what's shared
  • ✓ Communication tips — how to speak to them
  • ✓ Sensory needs — what to avoid, what helps
  • ✓ Emergency contact — always available
  • ✓ No app required — works on any smartphone
  • ✓ Looks like a normal bracelet — dignity, not diagnosis
